Industrial Equipment Warranty Resolution

A customer's equipment fails and this process gets it running again. Warranty, engineering, parts, field service, and finance all work the same claim, so diagnosis, containment, restoration, and settlement move together. Telemetry and service records tell the adjuster what the machine did before anyone rules on coverage. Closing the claim records what the failure should change.

Agent and human work

Potential agent steps and the decisions that remain with accountable people.

Agent opportunities

  • Summarize incident signals, impacted services, and likely causes from live operational context.
  • Route exceptions by likely root cause and recommended resolution path.
  • Coordinate investigation, resolution, and communication tasks across the full case lifecycle.

Human review

  • What constitutes critical customer impact for each product and service model?
  • Which evidence is required before each coverage outcome can be authorized?
  • How do third-party replacements and controls changes affect coverage, and when is causation review required?
  • Which containment actions may proceed while coverage remains undecided?
