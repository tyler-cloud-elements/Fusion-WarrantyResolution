The trade to weigh

A cap helps the reading pages and can hurt the table. Six columns at 960px is tight — Why it's here and Description start wrapping to three lines where today they fit on one or two. Toggle the switch and watch those two columns.

That is the argument for scoping the flag to card-based pages — the decision consoles and case detail — and leaving the queue's table full-bleed, rather than one cap over everything. It is one line either way; it just needs deciding, because "all the cards" and "all the pages" are not the same set.
