Surface: Submit fill:

Round two: more surface contrast, and a submit button that passes AA

Two separate problems with two different standards. The surface question is taste and perceptibility; the button question is a measured WCAG AA text failure. Every number here was computed by compositing the real token values.

1 · More separation between sections and the page

We are at card 1.055 / rim 1.106 today. The non-obvious part: as the page darkens the fill contrast rises but the rim contrast falls, because --border is a fixed light grey that converges on a darkening page. So going further means moving both — darken the page and take the rim from border-border/60 to the full token.

S2 is the pick. With the rim at full strength it lands at fill 1.102 / rim 1.138 — both inside the 1.05–1.15 band where adjacent surfaces read as separate, and neither pushed so far that the page starts to look grey. S3 buys a stronger fill but the rim slips under S2's, and the canvas begins to read as a different colour rather than a lighter shade of the same one. S1 is the option if this should barely change.

Still not a WCAG number. 1.4.11's 3:1 governs the boundary of a control, not the layering of panels. These are perceptibility figures.

2 · The submit button — this one is a real AA failure

White ink on --primary (#009fb0) measures 3.19:1. The label is 13px / 600, which is normal text under WCAG 1.4.3 — large text starts at 18.66px bold — so the requirement is 4.5:1. It fails, and it fails for the one control that files the decision.

SubmitBar.tsx already records the 3.19 and guesses that --primary-700 "would clear it". Measured, it does not: 700 is 3.65. The first rung that clears 4.5 is 800.

800 is the recommendation — the first rung that passes, still unmistakably the brand teal, and only two steps from where it is. 900 passes with more headroom but reads closer to navy than to the product's accent, and it would make the primary button darker than any other filled control in the app.

The same flaw is in the sibling filled variants

Not what was asked, but it is the same measurement and worth knowing before choosing scope — white ink on each filled button/badge token:

Every filled variant fails AA with white ink, and warning is not close — 1.89 is barely legible. The plan proposes fixing primary now, in the default button variant, and treats the family as a separate follow-up so this change stays reviewable.
