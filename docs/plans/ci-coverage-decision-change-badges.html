The hue is the SECOND cue, on purpose. The three washes are near-identical in luminance — + against ○ is 1.06:1 — so they differ in hue alone. In greyscale, or to a reader with a red-green deficiency, the fills are the same: + ○ − — and the glyph still answers. Asking the colour to carry the meaning alone would have been the defect; the glyph you asked for is what prevents it.

3 · Two glyph choices

You wrote O. At 11px a capital O is a beat from a zero and sits on a different optical weight from + and −, which are both symmetrical strokes. Either is one character.
