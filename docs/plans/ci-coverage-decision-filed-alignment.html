Filed record — put back in the live card's order and dress

Filing the decision currently re-orders the card and re-draws four of its sections in markup of their own, so a restyle of the live card does not reach the record. The record becomes the same sections, in the same sequence, rendered by the same components in a read-only mode — evidence, then amount and rationale, then the resolution, then what changed.

1 · The order, measured on the running page

Both columns are probed off the live DOM, not read off the source. The record does not just differ — it runs the body backwards, so the three things a reviewer reads top-to-bottom before deciding come back bottom-to-top after.

What changed stays last. It is the only block with no counterpart in the live card, and its own note already argues the position: the values as signed, then the commentary on how they got that way. It is also the one block that stays outside the aria-readonly wrapper, because it is not one of the fields.

The dimming survives; the fill does not. Keeping the unpicked cards at 45% is the record's own good idea — "one of three" is part of what was decided — so it stays, as a frozen state of the real component. The bg-primary/5 goes: choicebox.tsx rejected a filled selection twice, once for looking like the Submit button and once because white on --primary is 3.19:1.

4 · The evidence columns, which do not line up with anything

evidenceColumns.ts exists precisely so that three things agree on these edges — the band's headings, every row, and the composer's line. The filed record is a fourth thing that does not read it.
