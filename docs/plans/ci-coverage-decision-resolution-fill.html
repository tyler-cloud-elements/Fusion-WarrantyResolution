Resolution cards — a non-primary fill, and a smaller box

Two answers. Yes, the app has a secondary filled button variant — but it cannot carry this selected state, and the numbers say why. And the cards are measurably bigger than Submit: 91px against 61px, on a 14px radius against 10px.

1 · Why bg-secondary cannot do it

--secondary is oklch(0.9593 …) and --muted is oklch(0.963 …). In light mode those are the same colour to three decimal places, so a selected bg-secondary card against unselected bg-muted ones separates at 1.01:1 — invisible.

The live teal fill fails AA. White on --primary in light mode is 3.19:1 — under the 4.5 that 13px semibold needs. That is a real defect in what I shipped an hour ago, and it is the strongest argument for changing the fill rather than just recolouring it for taste.

3 · bg-muted-foreground — the recommendation

A filled slate, from the same family as the unselected bg-muted ground — so the selected card reads as the same control filled in, rather than as a different-coloured object. 7.15:1 ink in both themes, 6.4:1 separation, and nothing about it competes with the teal action.

4 · The size — 91px down to about 63px

The type was never bigger: the title is already 13/600, exactly Submit's label, and the description 12px, exactly Submit's payload line. What made them loom is the box — 14px padding all round, a 14px radius against Submit's 10, and a 16px marker. Both come down a rung, and the type follows so the action is unambiguously the loudest thing.
